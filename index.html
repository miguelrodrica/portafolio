About Me

I'm a Data Analyst with a Business Administration background, currently deepening my expertise in Data Engineering. My work sits at the intersection of business logic and technical execution: I design SQL data models, build Python pipelines and deliver Power BI dashboards that help teams make decisions with confidence.

Over the past months I've worked on end-to-end analytics projects — from raw data ingestion and ETL pipelines to interactive dashboards and production-ready applications — always focused on writing clean, maintainable code and communicating findings clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
